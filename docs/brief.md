# Project brief

**From:** Head of Customer Retention
**To:** Nicolas, Analytics
**Subject:** Q-next retention campaign: who should we call?

Hi Nicolas,

We lost about a quarter of our customer base last quarter, and the board wants to know what we're doing about it.

Next quarter I have budget for a proactive retention campaign. The retention team will contact customers and offer them a $100 account credit to stay. We have capacity to contact 1,000 customers at most, and I'd rather contact fewer if that makes more financial sense. From past campaigns, we think the offer keeps roughly 30% of customers who were genuinely going to leave. Customers who were going to stay anyway still take the credit, so that money is wasted.

**My question:** Which customers should we target to protect the most revenue, and is the campaign worth running at all?

**What I need from you:**
1. A way to score every current customer on their likelihood of leaving. I need to trust it and understand it. I don't want a black box, so please tell me what's driving churn.
2. A recommended target list and size. How many customers should we contact, and which ones? I care about revenue at risk, not just headcount.
3. The expected financial outcome. Roughly how much revenue do we save, what does the campaign cost, and what's the net?
4. Two or three insights the product team can act on (pricing, contract structure, etc.).

**Constraint:** we'll run your model at the START of the quarter, on customers who haven't left yet. It can only use information we'd genuinely have at that point.

**Deliverable:** ~10-slide presentation for a non-technical audience, technical detail in an appendix.
